## Review setup

## Reviewer 1 - technical validity / technical failings emphasis

- Overall assessment: The paper has a coherent technical story and a much cleaner evidence boundary than earlier annotation-assisted variants. The deployable/no-GT distinction is now explicit, and the main counts are auditable. The case is not yet fully submission-ready because some reproducibility and failure-taxonomy artifacts remain reconstructed or manuscript-authoritative rather than independently generated.
- Who would be interested in the results, and why: Researchers working on VLM-assisted manipulation, language-conditioned grasping, and evaluation protocols for embodied VLM pipelines would care because the work separates target selection, blocker reasoning, and mask success rather than reporting a single opaque answer accuracy.
- Major strengths: controlled 900-sample same-cache comparison; explicit SSR/RSR metric separation; S1 treated as oracle only; provenance note avoids pretending a missing console log exists; failure analysis identifies blocker visibility as the true hard-scene bottleneck.
- Major concerns: the main run manifest records dirty_worktree=true; the raw run_log.txt is absent; the semantic six-class failure taxonomy is not regenerated by a standalone script; split-0 ablations can be overread if the paper does not repeatedly mark them as mechanism checks rather than full-run evidence.
- Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established: add or cite a script that regenerates the failure taxonomy from saved rows, tighten provenance language around dirty-worktree reproducibility, and ensure the manuscript never claims real-robot success or latency from offline call counts.
- Assessment against review axes: originality is credible as a decision-contract/routing contribution; significance is field-local but useful; interdisciplinary reach is limited unless the introduction better explains why blocker reasoning is a general embodied-AI issue; technical soundness is mostly good for offline claims but not for embodied execution claims; readability is acceptable for robotics readers but dense for nonspecialists.
- Recommendation posture: potentially suitable after major internal revision, with the largest required fixes around artifact reproducibility and claim boundaries.

## Reviewer 2 - originality / significance emphasis

- Overall assessment: The main contribution is not a new foundation model or grasp planner, but a disciplined routing and auditing protocol for an existing FreeGrasp-style pipeline. That is a plausible RA-L contribution if framed as a practical reliability/evaluation advance rather than a broad solution to robotic grasping.
- Who would be interested in the results, and why: Readers interested in making VLM manipulation systems inspectable would value the decomposition into evidence packets, route guards, consensus, and mask repair. The separated oracle result also helps quantify how much headroom remains if visibility and blocker evidence improve.
- Major strengths: the paper repositions previous annotation-assisted experiments as diagnostics; it reports controlled S0 rather than relying only on prior-paper GPT-4o rates; it distinguishes descriptive prior-paper comparisons from paired same-cache comparisons.
- Major concerns: the novelty over structured prompting, Set-of-Mark prompting, and existing FreeGrasp/ThinkGrasp needs sharper articulation. Some related-work claims are plausible but not yet source-verified. The stated performance gains are meaningful mainly in SSR; the paper should avoid sounding as if it solved reasoning broadly.
- Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established: verify every related-work citation and baseline value; remove uncited bibliography ballast or connect it to specific claims; make the abstract and conclusion say that the strongest controlled evidence is SSR, with RSR as a cautious positive trend.
- Assessment against review axes: originality is moderate and depends on the auditable routing framing; scientific importance is strongest for VLM manipulation reliability, not robotics broadly; interdisciplinary interest is currently modest; technical soundness is enough for offline benchmark claims; readability would improve with a clearer one-sentence problem framing for non-FreeGrasp readers.
- Recommendation posture: promising but must sharpen novelty and citation support before being sent as a confident submission.

## Reviewer 3 - readability / broader readership emphasis

- Overall assessment: The manuscript has the right pieces, but a new reader must absorb many project-specific names: EGHC3_v3, S0, S1, v10, RSR, SSR, FreeGraspData, route guard, mask repair, and Molmo cache. The current paper is understandable to project insiders; it needs a gentler narrative bridge for reviewers who have not followed the experiment history.
- Who would be interested in the results, and why: Robotics and embodied-AI readers who worry about opaque VLM decisions would be interested in the error localization story: easy failures are often mask/instance issues, while hard failures are blocker/visibility issues.
- Major strengths: the abstract now states the main result and caveats; the limitations paragraph is unusually honest; the failure-analysis material can become a strong discussion section because it explains what still fails rather than only celebrating aggregate gains.
- Major concerns: the phrase  our previous annotation-assisted may create double-blind concerns and also pulls readers into project history too early. The manuscript should replace project-internal chronology with neutral terminology such as prior diagnostic variants where possible. Figures use Type 3 fonts in current PDF metadata, which may cause submission-quality concerns.
- Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established: improve figure/font readiness, remove self-identifying phrasing, add a short explanation of RSR/SSR before the abstract’s dense numeric paragraph if the format allows, and ensure all table captions state whether a row is full-protocol, split-0, descriptive, controlled, or oracle.
- Assessment against review axes: originality can be understood if framed around auditable decision contracts; significance is not yet made broad; interdisciplinary readability is the weakest axis; technical claims are mostly bounded; the manuscript would benefit from reducing acronyms and moving some provenance detail to supplement.
- Recommendation posture: revise for clarity and double-blind robustness before external review.

## Cross-review synthesis

- Consensus strengths: the work now has a defensible deployable/no-GT main method, controlled same-cache baseline, explicit metric contract, and a transparent discussion of remaining hard-scene blocker failures.
- Consensus technical risks: dirty-worktree provenance, missing raw run log, missing standalone taxonomy regeneration, split-0 ablation overgeneralization, and absent real-robot validation.
- Where emphasis differs: Reviewer 1 weights reproducibility and artifact validity most; Reviewer 2 weights novelty and citation support; Reviewer 3 weights readability, double-blind wording, and submission formatting.
- Broad-interest / significance readout: the paper is strongest as a robotics/VLM reliability contribution. It is not yet positioned as a broad embodied intelligence result because evidence remains offline and task-specific.
- Most important issues to resolve before a strong submission case is established: finish citation metadata verification, add/freeze a taxonomy regeneration artifact or clearly label taxonomy provenance, revise self-referential wording, fix Type 3 figure fonts if the venue disallows them, and keep all real-world claims out unless validation is added.

## Risk / unsupported claims

- Wall-clock latency or deployment-time cost: unsupported beyond VLM call counts.
- Clean-checkout reproducibility of the main run: unsupported because manifest records dirty worktree and raw console run log is absent.
- Strong RSR improvement claim: weak; state as smaller positive delta with CI lower bound at 0.00 pp.
- Related-work statements about exact model capabilities or failure modes: require source-level verification before final submission.
